Why it's more likely tonight than usual

Each bar is the chance Denver hits one mark, from both teams' 2026 form. The black tick is the league average.

Multiplying those four together gives about 1.9%. The real rate is higher, because good defensive nights come in bunches: a pass rush forces bad throws, and a lead forces the other team to drop back more. Across the league, multiplying says 0.69% while the real rate is 1.98%, so the levers cluster about 2.9×. Applying that to tonight gives 5.3%, about one game in 19.
